# Amann & Escher: Analysis I–III — Exercise Solutions

An independently written, unofficial companion for working through the exercises in Herbert Amann and Joachim Escher’s three-volume introduction to analysis.

The solutions emphasize the definitions and logical steps needed to reach each result. Mathematical expressions use GitBook-compatible LaTeX notation. Short descriptions identify the exercises; the textbook itself remains the source for their complete statements.

## Browse the volumes

- [Analysis I](analysis-i/README.md) — Foundations, convergence, continuity, and differentiation. Chapter I, Chapter II, Sections 1–9, Chapter III, Sections 1–6, Chapter IV, Sections 1–2, and Chapter V, Sections 1–4, are solved.
- [Analysis II](analysis-ii/README.md) — Chapter VI, Sections 1–9, Chapter VII, Sections 1–10, and Chapter VIII, Sections 1–6: exercises addressed.
- [Analysis III](analysis-iii/README.md) — Chapter IX, Sections 1–4: Exercises 1–19, 1–13, 1–11, and 1–10 addressed, respectively.
